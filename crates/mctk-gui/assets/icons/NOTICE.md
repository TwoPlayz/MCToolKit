# Icon provenance

These PNGs were extracted from the embedded Qt resources of Universal Minecraft Tool 1.5.10
(`UniversalMinecraftTool.exe`, paths `/img/icons`, `/img/icons/tags`,
`/img/icons/pruner_thumbnails`, `/img/platforms`) and renamed (`TAG_`, `THUMB_` and
`PLATFORM_` prefixes). The project owner states they are free to use.

Deliberately **not** included:

- UMT's own branding: the application logo, the NBT Editor, Converter and Pruner app icons,
  and the extension thumbnail derived from the logo. MCToolKit draws its own.
- Minecraft artwork (Mojang): the player face, map item, pack image and water/lava textures.

Many of the general icons (folder, file, save, search, settings, trash, info, cloud, undo/redo,
user) match Font Awesome Free, whose icons are licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://fontawesome.com/license/free). The platform marks identify Xbox 360, PlayStation 3,
Wii U, Windows/Bedrock and Java Edition; they are trademarks of their owners and are used only
to label those editions.
